# DESIGN.md - NekoFrost Visual & Creative Direction

## 1. Brand Identity
* **Name**: NekoFrost
* **Vibe & Character**: Playful, warm, appetizing, and cute Japanese-inspired fast-casual food brand.
* **Mascot / Icon**: Curled white fluffy cat with blue outline and blush pink cheeks.
* **Tagline**: Crispy Bite, Big Happiness.

---

## 2. Color System
* **Cream (`#F1EDE1`)**: Primary canvas/background color. Warm, clean, non-sterile.
* **Sea Breeze (`#2B5EA9`)**: Primary brand color. Used for category bars, brand text, dark sheets, and primary buttons.
* **MPink (`#EBA9B5`)**: Accent highlight color. Used for cart badges, promo badges (*Hemat Rp 6.000*), heart/favorite icons, and subtle active underlines.
* **Deep Contrast (`#1E447C`)**: Secondary deep navy for sheet contrast and text hierarchy.

---

## 3. UI & UX Principles
* **Anti-Slop Guardrails**:
  - No generic purple AI gradients.
  - No overused cookie-cutter corporate cards.
  - High contrast for accessibility and readability.
  - Tactile physical buttons and thoughtful micro-interactions.
* **Layout**:
  - Circular top-down food dishes with soft depth shadows.
  - 2-column mobile layout, 4-column desktop layout.
  - Guest checkout without mandatory login friction.
  - Smooth WhatsApp order confirmation integration.
